Umbral Ascent: privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Umbral Ascent is published by SNI Gaming.

What the game itself stores

  • Umbral Ascent has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • Your progress is saved in a file on your own device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Adverts

The game shows an advert only when you choose to watch one in exchange for a gift. Adverts are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is worked out,
  • information about the device and about how you interact with adverts in the game.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your choice about personalised adverts before any advert is loaded. You can change that choice at any time under Menu, Advert choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the game never sees your card or account details. The game only learns which items you own, so that it can give them to you.

Children

Umbral Ascent is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date before the change takes effect.
