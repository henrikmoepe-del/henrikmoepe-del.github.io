Rampart Support

Rampart: Idle Castle Defense, for Android and iPhone.

Contact

Write to henrikmoepe@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we will answer as soon as we can.

A purchase did not arrive

Open the game with an internet connection: unfinished purchases are picked up when the game starts. On iPhone you can also use Settings, Restore Purchases. If it still has not arrived, write to us with the date of the purchase.

My progress

The game has no account. Your progress is saved on your phone, and your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup or Android backup) can bring it back on a new phone. Uninstalling the game without a backup deletes it.

No ad is ready

Ads need an internet connection and are not always available. Nothing is used up when no ad is shown; try again a little later.
